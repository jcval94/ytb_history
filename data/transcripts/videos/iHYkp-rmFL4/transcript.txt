The World Cup in North America will have fans fly between 16 host cities spread across three countries. While FIFA have promised to reduce and compensate the greenhouse gas emissions associated with the FIFA World Cups. Germany supporters can expect to rack up some serious air miles. A fan flying from, say, Frankfurt and attending Germany's three group stage matches will travel nearly 7,000 miles, generating major emissions. There are fans who just want to see their team.
