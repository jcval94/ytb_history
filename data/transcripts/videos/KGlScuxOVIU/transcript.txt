These are the craziest death rituals around the world. So in Madagascar, upon dying, the public exhumes the corpses and wraps them in silk shrouds and dances with them to live music as part of their Damadihana ritual, meant to be a joyful celebration of the life of the deceased. In the high altitudes of Tibet, bodies are placed on a mountain peak to be consumed by vultures as a final act of generosity to the world to sustain other living beings. The Tana Toraja people of Indonesia will actually live alongside the bodies of their deceased loved ones inside of their homes for months or even years, bringing them food, a change of clothes, and even talking to them. Also in Indonesia is the Ikipali tribe, which amputates their own fingers when a close relative dies in order to appease the spirits and represent their emotional suffering. The Yanomami people of the Amazon rainforest cremate bodies and then drink their bone ash in order to keep their spirit alive within themselves. And finally, the Igorot people of the Philippines vertically bury loved ones off the side of limestone cliffs because the higher the coffin, the closer the soul is to the ancestral spirits. Pretty cool, right?
