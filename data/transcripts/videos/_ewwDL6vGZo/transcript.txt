What is the funniest movie of all time? So, based on an American Film Institute poll which polled 1,800 actors, directors, and critics, the funniest movie is Some Like It Hot from 1959, which they claim has a nearly perfect script and has stood the test of time. If we're judging off of the highest rated comedy film, though, based on Rotten Tomatoes and IMDb, then Monty Python and the Holy Grail has the highest critic and audience score at 95% each. However, if we're determining the funniest movie based on quotable lines, then it's actually a tie between Anchorman and Mean Girls, both of which have about a dozen iconic lines each. In terms of rewatchability, Superbad is actually the funniest as the comedy doesn't worsen on the second watch, and the funniest movie on the first watch is apparently The Hangover, achieving one of the highest viewer satisfaction scores. But technically, in terms of joke density, the funniest movie of all time is Airplane with three jokes per minute, as the movie is literally engineered to be funny by utilizing the background to show a visual joke while the verbal jokes are happening, apparently never giving the audience a chance not to laugh.
