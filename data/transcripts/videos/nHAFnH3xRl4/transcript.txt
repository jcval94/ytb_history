Could identical twins get away with murder? Identical twins have the same DNA, so if one of them commits a murder, which one goes to prison? When police run a standard DNA test at a crime scene and it matches two twins, they're stuck. And this has literally happened. In Germany, two twin brothers were suspected of robbing luxury stores. DNA was found, but they both walked free because they couldn't prove who did it. But the identical in identical twins isn't quite right. Monozygotic twins start with the same DNA, but they don't stay identical. Every time your cells divide, copying errors can occur and a single letter in your genetic code can get swapped. Across three billion base pairs, twins may differ in roughly five positions. Now, in the unique case of twin suspects, scientists can now use whole genome sequencing, which reads all three billion base pairs to try and find the differences, but these are expensive and sufficient DNA samples are required to ensure results.
